Download the App

Get the latest version of the African Shipping app for your Android device.

Download App

How to Download:

  1. Click the "Download App" button above.
  2. Your browser will download the `app-debug.apk` file.
  3. Once downloaded, tap on the file to begin the installation.
  4. If prompted, you may need to allow installation from "Unknown Sources" in your device's security settings.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

Update Your App

Ensure you have the latest features and bug fixes by updating your application.

How to Update:

  1. **Check for Updates:** The app may notify you if a new version is available. You can also always check this page for the latest version.
  2. **Download New Version:** Click the "Download App" button in the section to the left to get the most recent `app-debug.apk` file.
  3. **Install Update:** Open the newly downloaded APK file. Your device will typically recognize it as an update to your existing app.
  4. **Confirm Installation:** Follow the prompts to confirm the update. Your existing app data will usually be preserved.
  5. **Launch App:** Once the update is complete, open the African Shipping app to enjoy the latest improvements!

Note: In rare cases, if you encounter issues, you might need to uninstall the old version before installing the new one. However, this is generally not required for updates.
