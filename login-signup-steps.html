Login & Sign Up Guide

How to Log In

  1. Open the App: Launch the African Shipping application on your mobile device.
  2. Find Login: Look for the "Login" or "Sign In" button on the welcome screen.
  3. Enter Credentials: Input your registered email address or username and your password into the respective fields.
  4. Submit: Tap the "Login" or "Sign In" button to access your account.
  5. Forgot Password?: If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login screen to reset it.

How to Sign Up

  1. Open the App: Launch the African Shipping application on your mobile device.
  2. Find Sign Up: Look for the "Sign Up", "Register", or "Create Account" button on the welcome screen.
  3. Provide Information: Fill in the required details such as your email address, and create a strong password.
  4. Agree to Terms: Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  5. Verify (if applicable): You will receive a verification email or SMS. Follow the instructions to verify your account.
  6. Complete Registration: Tap the "Register" button to finalize your account creation.
  7. Login: Enter your email and passowrd and tap login to proceed to the home page
